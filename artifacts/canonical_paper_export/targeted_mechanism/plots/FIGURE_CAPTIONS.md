# Figure captions

All panels are separate vector PDFs. Whiskers show sampling uncertainty only.

## main\covariance_controls_scaffold_coordinate_1.pdf

SCAFFOLD, coordinate 1: unconstrained intercepts of the normalized balance observable under symmetric noise at the three covariance interventions. Filled circles use the full frozen grid; open squares omit only the largest step size. Whiskers are approximate 95% simultaneous sampling intervals for each analysis's 36-summary family; crosses are analytical targets. The intervals do not cover finite-grid remainder or burn-in error. The null is a normalized coefficient null, not a zero mean.

## main\covariance_matched_laws_coordinate_1.pdf

Coordinate 1: symmetric-minus-asymmetric fitted coefficient differences at rho=+1/4, where the two noise laws have identical population mean and covariance. Difference denotes the matched SCAFFOLD-minus-FedAvg observable. Whiskers are approximate 95% simultaneous intervals across the 36-summary family. Full and prespecified restricted grids are both shown. Green bands mark the frozen practical resolution margins (0.011875, 0.0075, 0.004375); crosses mark zero joint-limit targets. Sampling intervals do not include deterministic grid remainder or burn-in error. This comparison is not an equivalence proof and does not rule out finite-N law dependence.

Whiskers are ordinary 95% marginal Student-t intervals from 12 independent paired runs, not simultaneous intervals. The horizontal dashed line is the joint-limit coefficient target.
