# Research roadmap

| Research question | Method | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-dimensional stochastic SCAFFOLD stationary bias | Independent chains, batch means, and prespecified gates | Publication settings and small-step confirmation |
| Matched FedAvg versus SCAFFOLD coefficient attribution | Common random numbers, paired stopping, and paired inference | Small-step convergence and fixed-step large-client extrapolation |
| Multidimensional homogeneous SCAFFOLD validation | Controlled vector recursion, exact targets, rotation, extrapolation, and coordinatewise inference | Engineering smoke tests and formal low- and higher-dimensional matrices |

Exact matrices and results are defined by workstream configurations, code, and manifests.
