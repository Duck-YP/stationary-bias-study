\section{Computational provenance and reproducibility}
\label{app:reproducibility}

The reported numerical results come from recorded experiment artifacts.
Production artifacts retain configuration snapshots, source-commit identifiers,
seed blocks, raw run/batch summaries, fitted coefficients, bootstrap outputs,
validity checks, and target-inclusion outcomes. Diagnostic artifacts retain
the corresponding audits and provenance records.
Table~\ref{tab:F-artifacts} identifies the studies and their seed blocks.
Artifact names are reproduced verbatim; terms such as \texttt{confirmation}
and \texttt{validation} are bookkeeping labels, not assessments of evidence.

Matched pairs receive identical noise blocks keyed by seed, round, client,
and local-step index, regardless of differences in their state trajectories.
The $H=4$ diagnostic reads saved primary and smaller-step paired artifacts;
it generates no new stationary production chains.
Saved audits verify the identity
$\widehat D=\widehat{\mathcal C}^{\Scaf}-\widehat{\mathcal C}^{\FedAvg}$
and the residual decomposition to machine precision.
The largest recorded control-sum error is $7.27\times10^{-12}$,
below $10^{-10}$; the fixed-seed reference-kernel audit reports zero discrepancy.
The four secondary direct--balance failures are retained in
\texttt{p3-d2-coefficient-primary} and Table~\ref{tab:E-direct-balance}.

\paragraph{Supplementary provenance.}
The supplementary study was frozen on 9 October 2026 under the identifier \nolinkurl{independent-supplement-v1}. It records 69 production configurations and 828 paired runs (seeds 20001--20012); pilot and affine checks are separate. Numerical kernels, objective, data-processing code and engine match retained frozen hashes. The source manifest distinguishes reporting/scheduling changes; four complete freeze-time files are unavailable, so the current source is not represented as a complete historical checkout.

The local \nolinkurl{supplementary_results/} directory supplies recorded tables, frozen settings, diagnostic summaries, manifests and the source-review snapshot. Data hashes and source URLs identify a9a from \url{https://www.csie.ntu.edu.tw/~cjlin/libsvmtools/datasets/binary.html}. Full production arrays, restart states and examples remain in the experiment archive.

The source manifest lists \url{https://github.com/Duck-YP/stationary-bias-study} at commit \texttt{7f6de2c}, with the supplementary directory untracked. That commit is therefore not a verified supplementary-code release. Reproduction requires the recorded source, environment, commands and experiment arrays. The analysis-only batch audit covers E1 and R0, not R1/R2 batches or raw-example task-loss regeneration. Historical studies remain separate, and all discrepancy records are preserved.
